1. Description of Services

SubHelp is an iOS application that lets you track subscriptions you enter yourself. You can:

  • View monthly and yearly spending totals, a calendar of dates you entered, and savings estimates calculated from those figures

You can turn off all App notifications, or choose how far in advance reminders appear, including an option not to receive those reminders while still allowing notification permission. The App offers a free tier, limited to 3 subscriptions you are currently tracking, and SubHelp Premium, which removes that limit.
