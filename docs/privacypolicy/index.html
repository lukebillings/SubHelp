Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

SubHelp ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our SubHelp mobile application ("App") on iOS devices, including the SubHelp Home Screen widget. This policy applies globally and includes provisions required under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and other jurisdictions with similar data protection laws.

The App does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not ask you to create a SubHelp account. Renewal and free-trial reminders (if you enable them) are local notifications scheduled on your device; they are not delivered from servers we operate.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Subscription data: Details you add or edit, including names, prices, billing frequencies, renewal or next-payment dates, categories, colours, notes, the date a subscription was added, and how you organise it on the in-app board (for example keep, look into cancelling, find another provider, or try to get cheaper). If you mark a subscription as a free trial, we also store that it is a trial and the trial end date you enter. This includes subscriptions you move to history when you cancel or stop tracking them.
  • Templates you confirm: The App can prefill common services, including example Apple services, with suggested names and prices. Those suggestions are stored only if you choose to add them, and only with the price, date, and other details you confirm. The App does not read your Apple subscriptions, bank accounts, or email receipts.
  • Device settings: Preferences stored on your device, such as currency, whether app notifications are enabled, how far in advance renewal and free-trial reminders should fire (or that you do not want those reminders while still allowing notification permission), haptic feedback on or off, and your SubHelp Premium entitlement state as recognised by the App (see purchases below). The App also stores on-device flags for onboarding, tutorials, and similar in-app prompts.
  • Contact communications: If you email us (for example, to request a feature, ask for help, or exercise privacy rights), we process your email address, message content, and any information you include in that request.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Purchase information: If you subscribe to SubHelp Premium, Apple processes payment and may make subscription status available to the App through StoreKit in accordance with Apple's App Store terms. If Apple offers an introductory price or free trial, Apple determines eligibility, when billing begins, renewal dates, and pricing; the App uses that information only to unlock SubHelp Premium features. We do not receive the list of subscriptions you track, your notes, or your renewal dates from Apple. Those details come only from what you enter.
  • Device information: The App runs on your device like any iOS app. We do not operate backend systems that collect device identifiers, usage logs, or analytics from the App. Optional prompts to rate or share the App use Apple’s system APIs. Whether you leave a review, and who you share a link with, is handled by Apple and by you.

1.3 Information We Do Not Access

We do not connect to your bank, email, or Apple subscription account. Links in Help and cancellation guides can open Apple’s subscription page, the Settings app, or a service provider’s website so you can check those sources yourself. What you do on those sites or in Settings is governed by that provider or by Apple, not by us. We do not receive a copy of those pages.

2. Data Storage

Your subscription data (subscriptions you track, your cancellation history, notes, board organisation, and trial details) and app settings are stored on your device using standard iOS storage (for example, UserDefaults).

The App uses Apple’s iCloud Key-Value Storage to sync your subscription list and in-app history between your own devices signed into the same Apple ID with iCloud enabled. Preferences such as currency, notification choices, and haptic feedback stay on each device.

If you add the SubHelp widget, the App copies the details needed to show upcoming payments into an App Group container on your device (shared only between the App and the SubHelp widget). That copy can include subscription names, prices, billing frequency, next payment or trial end dates, whether an item is a free trial, and your currency. It is not uploaded to servers we operate.

We do not operate our own servers for storing your subscription list. iCloud sync is provided by Apple; Apple’s privacy policy and iCloud terms apply to data processed through iCloud: apple.com/legal/privacy. If iCloud is turned off or unavailable, the App continues to work with data stored locally on the device. The widget then shows whatever copy is on that device.

Local notifications, while pending, are stored by iOS on your device. Their text can include the subscription name, price, and renewal or trial date you entered.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App's core functionality: tracking active and historical subscriptions, organising them on the board, calculating spend and savings estimates from the figures you enter, showing cancellation help, and showing upcoming payments in the App and widget.
  • To schedule local renewal reminders and free-trial reminders on your device when you allow notifications. A trial reminder can include actions such as recording that you kept or cancelled that trial. Those actions update data on your device.
  • To respect your choices for notifications (including turning off renewal and trial reminders only) and optional haptic feedback.
  • To process and recognise your SubHelp Premium entitlement via Apple’s in-app purchase system, including restoring a previous purchase.
  • To respond to emails and support requests you send us, including feature requests and privacy queries.
  • To improve the App and fix technical issues (for example through App Store reviews or support contact you initiate).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Savings figures, suggested prices, and cancellation guides are calculated or written for convenience. They are not a record we collect from your bank or from the services you subscribe to.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the EEA, UK, and other GDPR-applicable jurisdictions, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide the App and SubHelp Premium services you have agreed to.
  • Legitimate interests: Improving our services, responding to user communications (including feature requests and support emails), preventing fraud, and ensuring security, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: Where applicable law requires it, for example when you grant system notification permission to Apple for the App to schedule local renewal and free-trial alerts.
  • Legal obligation: Where we must process data to comply with applicable law.

5. Your Rights (Including GDPR Rights)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction: Request that we limit processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Complain: Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

You can edit or delete individual subscriptions in the App. Reset all data in Settings removes subscriptions, history, and most App preferences (including currency, notification choices, reminder timing, and haptic settings) from that device, removes the copies the App wrote to iCloud Key-Value Storage, and refreshes the on-device widget snapshot. Reset does not cancel SubHelp Premium with Apple, delete email you have sent us, or remove the App. Deleting the App removes its local data and widget data on that device. Data in your iCloud account is managed by Apple and may persist according to your Apple ID and iCloud settings until the App replaces or removes the keys it wrote, or you manage storage through your Apple account. We do not maintain a separate copy of your subscription list on our systems. To exercise other rights, or to request a copy of any data we hold (outside what is on your device and Apple systems), please contact us using the details below.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide the App and fulfil the purposes described in this policy. Locally stored data, including the widget snapshot and pending local notifications, remains on your device until you delete or edit it, use in-app reset, turn reminders off, or delete the App. Synced subscription data in iCloud Key-Value Storage remains according to Apple's retention and your iCloud account settings, until the App removes those keys or you remove them through Apple. Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to respond, improve the App, and meet legal requirements. For purchase and account-related data processed by Apple, retention follows Apple’s policies and applicable law (for example tax and dispute resolution).

7. International Transfers

Where your data is stored in iCloud, it may be processed on Apple’s infrastructure in various countries in accordance with Apple’s terms and privacy policy. If we transfer any personal data we hold (such as support email) outside the EEA, UK, or your home jurisdiction, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (for example standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions) as required by applicable law, including GDPR.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. Data stored on your device, including the App Group used by the widget, is protected by your device's security (for example passcode or biometrics). When iCloud sync is used, Apple secures data in transit and at rest according to Apple's security practices. We do not receive or host a copy of your subscription list on infrastructure we operate. You are responsible for keeping your Apple ID and device secure.

10. Third-Party Services

The App may integrate with or link to:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: For SubHelp Premium purchases, restores, and optional rating prompts. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Apple iCloud (Key-Value Storage): To sync your subscription data between your devices linked to your Apple ID. Subject to Apple's iCloud and privacy terms.
  • Apple widgets: The SubHelp widget reads the on-device App Group snapshot described above. Apple’s widget and system software display that information on your Home Screen.
  • Apple User Notifications (local notifications): If you enable reminders, the App schedules renewal and free-trial notifications on your device using dates you provide. They are not push messages from our servers. Apple’s notification permission and settings apply.
  • Links you choose to open: Help content, cancellation guides, Apple subscription management, and Share may open Apple or third-party pages. Those services process information under their own privacy policies once you leave the App.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via the App or by other reasonable means, including by updating this page. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For questions, to exercise your rights, or to lodge a complaint, please contact us:

  • By email: subhelp@outlook.com
  • Via the in-app support or contact option, if available

If you are in the EEA or UK and have concerns we cannot resolve, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (for example the ICO in the UK, CNIL in France, or your national data protection authority).
